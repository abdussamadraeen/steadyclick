# SteadyClick

A lightweight, private browser extension that makes web browsing easier for people with hand tremors, low vision, or sensory sensitivity.

---

## Features

* **Steady Pointer:** Smooths out mouse shakes and tremors so you can point with precision.
* **Magnetic Click:** Expands clickable areas around buttons and links so you never miss a click.
* **Enhanced Contrast:** Makes faint or hard-to-read text sharp, clear, and easy to read.
* **Calm Display:** Freezes flashing banners and auto-playing videos to prevent distraction and eye strain.
* **Plain Language:** Highlights complicated medical or legal terms with helpful notes.

---

## Privacy First

* **100% Offline:** Runs entirely on your computer using a fast, self-contained engine.
* **Zero Tracking:** SteadyClick does not collect, track, or transmit any personal information or browsing history.

---

## How to Install

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open your browser and go to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions` in Microsoft Edge).
3. Turn on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `extension` folder.
5. Pin SteadyClick to your toolbar for easy access.
